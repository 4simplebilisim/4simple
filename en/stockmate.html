Key Features

What Can You Do with StockMate?

Comprehensive modules that digitize your warehouse operations end-to-end.

Warehouse Design & Location

Create your warehouse layout digitally. Know every product location with shelf, aisle, and area-based location mapping.

Barcode & RFID Integration

Real-time stock tracking with barcode and RF-ID readers. Error-free operations in receiving, counting, and shipping.
