Cookie Policy

Which cookies exist on this site, how long they last, and how to change your choice. Short answer: the site needs no cookies to work.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

1. In short

This site needs no cookies to work. It writes no cookie of its own accord, loads no third-party script, and uses no analytics or advertising tool. The only cookie we write records the choice you make in the cookie bar.

In a measurement taken from the browser's network and cookie lists on 1 November 2026, a first-time visitor to the site had no cookies created and no request left the site's own domain.

2. What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file saved to your browser when you visit a site. On your next visit the site can read it and, for example, remember your preferences. Local storage (localStorage) similarly keeps data in your browser.

4. Third-party requests

The site loads every resource, font files included, from its own domain. The typeface used to be served from Google Fonts; the font files were moved onto the site so that a visitor's IP address is not sent to Google. As of today, opening a page sends no request to any address other than 4simple.com.tr.

Vercel Inc., the infrastructure the site is hosted on, keeps server access logs as a technical necessity (IP address, date, browser information). These are not cookies; details are in our Privacy Notice.

5. Local storage

This site writes nothing to your browser's local storage (localStorage, sessionStorage). Because our domain served a different application in the past, older records may still exist in your browser; these can be removed by clearing site data in your browser settings.

6. Managing your choice

You can change your cookie choice in two ways:

  • by clicking the Cookie Preferences link in the footer of every page, which reopens the preference panel,
  • by deleting or blocking cookies in your browser settings, in which case the cookie bar appears again on your next visit.

No non-essential cookie or script runs before you consent. Choosing “Reject” does not prevent you from using any part of the site; rejecting is offered just as easily as accepting.

7. Changes

When we add a new tool to the site we update this table, and when a new category comes into use we ask for your choice again. The date of the last update appears at the top of this page.
