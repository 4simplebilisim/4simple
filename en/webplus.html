Key Features

Digitize Your Sales & Distribution Processes

WebPlus is a simplified version of Univera's enterprise solution EnRoute, designed for SMEs. It empowers your sales team to work effectively in the field.

Order Management

Real-time order entry from the field, order approval workflows, order history tracking, and customer-specific price lists. Your sales team can create orders from their mobile devices.

Route Planning & Tracking

Plan daily visit routes for your sales teams and track completed visits in real-time on the map. Save time and fuel with efficient route optimization.

Dealer & Distributor Management

Monitor the sales performance of your dealers and distributors from a single screen. Channel-based analysis, current account tracking, and partner management.

Sales Reporting & Analytics

Real-time sales dashboards, region-based performance reports, product analysis, and trend tracking. Make data-driven decisions and optimize your sales strategy.

Mobile Sales Team

Let your sales representatives instantly access the product catalog, stock status, and customer information from their mobile devices. Uninterrupted field operations with offline support.

ERP Integration

Seamless integration with your existing accounting and ERP systems. Data synchronization with Logo, Mikro, SAP, and similar systems. No more double data entry.
